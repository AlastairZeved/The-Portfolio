# DECISIONS.md — The Portfolio

The rulings of record for this repository. Append-only, numbered `B1` and up;
the later ruling wins. Every entry is a transcription of an owner statement
and carries a verbatim `Source:` line. An entry without a `Source:` line is
not law — it is an agent invention and any QA pass may delete it on sight
(convention adopted from TheBoards, its issue #207). Agents transcribe owner
decisions; they never author, amend, or extend them.

**Held pending owner confirmation, NOT law:** the two rulings formerly carried
in `README.md` — "no monospace, anywhere" and "fonts are self-hosted" — await
the owner's ratifying comment on
[issue #49](https://github.com/AlastairZeved/The-Portfolio/issues/49). Until
then they have no entry here. They are argued, with citations, in
`docs/PRD.md`'s out-of-scope table.
